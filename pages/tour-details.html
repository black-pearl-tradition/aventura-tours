A sample itinerary

Seven days in the wild

This sample route combines Uganda's forest and savannah experiences. Your final route and permits depend on availability and the dates you choose.

Day 1 · Arrive in Entebbe

Meet your local trip host, settle in, and go over the days ahead.

Day 2 · Travel to the western highlands

Take a scenic drive through changing landscapes, with stops along the way and an overnight stay near the forest.

Day 3 · Bwindi forest

Join a guided rainforest trek in search of mountain gorillas. Trek length and sightings vary with wildlife and trail conditions.

Day 4 · Community and recovery day

Enjoy a slower day for a community-led cultural visit, a nature walk, or time at your lodge.

Day 5 · Queen Elizabeth National Park

Continue to the savannah for an afternoon game drive and an evening at your lodge.

Day 6 · Wildlife and the Kazinga Channel

Spend the day on a game drive and a boat trip, with opportunities to see wildlife along the water.

Day 7 · Return to Entebbe

Travel back across the countryside for your onward flight or an extra night in the city.

Prepare for your trip

  • Gorilla trekking permits are limited, date-dependent, and confirmed separately.
  • Trek duration and terrain vary; bring sturdy walking shoes, rain protection, and a small day pack.
  • Tell us about mobility, dietary, or accommodation preferences when you inquire.
  • Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed and depend on natural conditions.

What your safari quote should confirm

The starting price is indicative. Before confirming, ask us for a written breakdown showing what applies to your dates and group, including transport, accommodation, meals, park entry, and gorilla permits. These items can affect the final price.
